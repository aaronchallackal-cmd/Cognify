How to understand your reaction time

Reaction time is not only about the brain. Your score can also be affected by attention, fatigue, distraction, mouse speed, screen refresh rate, browser delay, and device performance.

Under 250 ms

Fast response for this browser based task.

250 to 350 ms

Common range for many online visual reaction time tasks.

350 to 500 ms

Slower response, possibly affected by focus, fatigue, or device delay.

Over 500 ms

Delayed for this task, but not a medical result or diagnosis.

Why scores vary

Browser based reaction time tests are useful for education, but they are less controlled than lab based testing. A computer, browser, monitor, mouse, trackpad, and testing environment can all influence the final number.

About the Science

What this project demonstrates

Reaction Time

Reaction time is the delay between a stimulus appearing and a person responding to it. In Cognify, the stimulus is the green box and the response is the click.

Attention

Focus can affect performance. If a user is distracted, tired, or clicking too early, their results may become slower or less consistent.

Consistency

Comparing fastest, slowest, and average reaction time helps show whether responses were stable across trials or varied from attempt to attempt.

Responsible Use

Educational limits

Cognify is meant to demonstrate cognitive science concepts through a simple web app. It should not be used to diagnose, screen, or treat any medical condition.

Cognify is an educational cognitive performance demo. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose ADHD, concussion, neurological disease, or any health condition. Results should be interpreted as a short browser based performance snapshot, not a clinical assessment.
