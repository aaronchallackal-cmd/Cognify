About Cognify

Cognify is a browser based cognitive performance project designed to demonstrate concepts such as reaction time, attention, and response consistency.

Educational disclaimer: Cognify is not a medical device and does not diagnose ADHD, concussion, neurological disease, or any health condition.

Built as an educational health tech and cognitive science project.
